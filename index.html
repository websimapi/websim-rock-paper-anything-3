How to Play Rock Paper Anything

Welcome to Rock Paper Anything, where creativity meets logic! Here's how to play:

  • Start by selecting an emoji from the options provided or use your saved objects.
  • Once the game begins, look at the current object displayed.
  • Think of something that could logically beat that object.
  • Type your answer in the input field. It can be an emoji or a word.
  • Click "Beat It!" or press Enter to submit your answer.
  • Our AI will evaluate if your object can logically beat the current one.
  • If your answer is accepted, you win the round!
  • The game continues with your object becoming the new one to beat.
  • Try to create the longest chain of winning objects!

Play Mode:

  • Click the "Play Mode" button to start a game with your created objects.
  • The computer will randomly select an object from your list.
  • Choose one of your objects to beat the computer's object.
  • The game will determine the winner based on the relationships you've created.

Remember:

  • Be creative but logical! The AI will check if your object can realistically beat the current one.
  • Your answer should have a clear and justifiable connection to beating the current object.
  • In Play Mode, you can repeat objects, allowing for more strategic gameplay.
  • Your objects are saved locally, allowing you to continue your game later or start a new one with your created objects!

Example: Rock ⛰️ → Dynamite 💣 → Water 💧 → Sun ☀️ → Black Hole 🕳️
