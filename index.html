How to Play Rock Paper Anything

Welcome to Rock Paper Anything, where creativity meets strategy!

  • Start by selecting an emoji from the selector to begin your chain.
  • Look at the current object shown in the game area.
  • Think of something that would logically defeat that object!
  • Enter your object (emoji or text) in the input field.
  • Our AI will decide if your object can logically beat the current one.
  • If successful, your object becomes the new one to beat!
  • Try to create the longest chain of winning objects possible.

Battle Mode:

  • Once you've built a collection of objects, click "Battle Mode"
  • The computer will select an object from your collection.
  • Choose one of your objects to counter the computer's choice.
  • Win by selecting objects that have proven relationships!

Remember:

  • Be creative but logical - the AI evaluates real-world relationships.
  • The more objects you create, the more interesting the game becomes!
  • Your collection is saved locally so you can continue playing later.

Example: Rock ⛰️ → Dynamite 💣 → Water 💧 → Sun ☀️ → Black Hole 🕳️
